# Market scan: who hires and funds AI-safety work (November 2026)

Written 2026-11-22/23 to choose research that organisations with budgets will value from Nov 2026 to Nov 2027. It complements [landscape-2026-11.md](landscape-2026-11.md), which covers the research frontier; this doc covers demand: open roles, stated agendas, money, and trends.

**How this was gathered**
- **Roles** come from the orgs' own job-board APIs (Greenhouse, Ashby, Lever, Gem, Workday) and careers pages, pulled on 2026-11-22. Careers pages are mostly JavaScript shells, so the APIs are the authoritative list. Quotes are verbatim from the postings.
- **Agendas and money** come from the orgs' and funders' own pages wherever they could be fetched.
- **Tags.** Anything not confirmed on a primary page is marked *(unverified)* or *(snippet only)*. Coefficient Giving's site blocks automated fetches, so its status is *snippet only*.
- **Spot checks.** I re-fetched a sample of figures before committing, and all of them matched:
  - Funders and government: the CAISSI page title, the EA Funds TAI Fund grant range, the BlueDot grant ceiling and the AISI Alignment Project status.
  - Labs: Anthropic's AI Monitoring salary and two OpenAI salaries.
  - Non-lab postings (title, pay and one quoted phrase each): Apollo Control and Apollo's monitoring agenda, Trajectory, Resolution, Transluce Frontline Evaluator, FAR Senior RE, METR Embedded Assessments, and Meridian's careers page.
- **Paper counts** come from OpenAlex, because the arXiv API was rate-limiting (HTTP 429). See §4 for caveats.

### 1.4 What recurs across orgs
1. **Monitoring agents is the single most common new job.**
   - Anthropic's AI Monitoring role, posted 2026-11-16.
   - OpenAI's Misalignment Monitoring Lead, CoT Monitorability and Safety Oversight roles.
   - Apollo's Control and Security & Control roles.
   - METR's Embedded Assessments, which "stress-test labs' internal agent monitoring".
   - Scale, FAR (probe-based monitors), Gray Swan and Meridian ("scanners and monitors").
   - The repeated phrasing is about *measuring* monitors: "evaluations that measure monitor effectiveness", "reliability of monitoring signals", "low false-positive rates".
2. **Evals engineering for agents.** Eval and benchmark work appears at 21 of 22 non-lab orgs and in 27 of 36 core lab roles. Inspect is the common substrate (AISI, Epoch, Meridian).
3. **Eval validity.**
   - "thinking twice before trusting a number" (Anthropic);
   - "ensure our benchmarks are valid" (Vals);
   - "evaluate the reliability of these evaluations" (Trajectory);
   - "construct, predictive, and statistical validity" (CAISSI);
   - "review and certify benchmarks" (Epoch).
4. **Transcript analysis at scale and embedded or incident assessments.** METR, Apollo and Transluce have each started embedded-at-a-lab programmes since October 2026; Irregular and Meridian also do transcript analysis.
5. **Using AI agents as a force multiplier is now a stated requirement.**
   - "Strong experience with AI coding agents" (Apollo);
   - "experience using AI coding tools" (AISI);
   - "review … work produced by AI agents" (Anthropic);
   - "use AI tools aggressively" (Resolution);
   - and Trajectory's whole role on automating safety research.
6. **Security and cyber skills** are in heavy demand (sandbox escape, exfiltration, offensive security), but they need a background Alex doesn't have.
7. **Public output is the stated entry ticket for non-PhDs.**
   - Apollo: "welcome self-taught candidates … independent research".
   - FAR: "a paper, a fellowship project, or substantive public writing".
   - AISI: "paper, preprint, open-source tool or blog post".
   - AIUC: "a benchmark others adopted".

### Paper volume
- **Method:**
  - OpenAlex counts of works whose title or abstract matches each query.
  - The windows are 1 Jan–22 Nov 2025 against the same span in 2026, so indexing lag biases 2026 *downward*.
  - The arXiv API returned HTTP 429 throughout, so I used OpenAlex instead. OpenAlex covers all venues, not just arXiv.
  - Exact queries are listed below.
- **Baseline:** works mentioning "large language model" grew **1.93×** (68,045 → 131,536).
- **Relative growth** = topic growth ÷ 1.93.

**Caveats on these counts**
- They are noisy keyword counts.
- "AI control" phrasing collides with control theory and networking, so that row is unreliable. A looser query gave 2.4×.
- An unquoted "scheming" query returned more than 100k hits and was discarded.
- **Precision check.** I sampled 10 random 2026 titles for three queries:
  - "reward hacking": mostly RL-method papers that mention it in passing. The term is spreading, but dedicated work is a minority.
  - LLM-as-a-judge: mostly applied uses of judges, not research on judges.
  - CoT monitorability: about half on-topic; the rest are other senses of "monitorability".
- So read the relative-growth column as "the term is spreading faster than the field", not as a count of dedicated papers.
- A second retry of the arXiv API also failed.

### Judgement by topic, combining hiring, agendas and papers
- **AI control and agent monitoring: the strongest signal in hiring and agendas; paper counts are inconclusive.**

# Mimir

An AI-safety research program, run as a research director working with teams of AI agents.

In Norse myth, Mímir's head is kept after his death, and Odin consults it for knowledge it holds but can't easily say. Interpretability and evals do something similar: they look inside a model to read what it knows.

## Layout

- `projects/` holds one directory per research project: its question, code, results and write-up.
- `research-log.md` records the decisions behind the work: what was tried, what was dropped, and why.
- `docs/` holds landscape scans, proposals and notes that span projects.
